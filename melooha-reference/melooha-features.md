## 3. Product Architecture & Monetization Strategy Summary

Melooha follows a **Freemium Utility-to-Consultation Funnel**:

1. **Top of Funnel (100% Free):**
   - High-SEO Vedic Calculators (12 tools running on Swiss Ephemeris).
   - Knowledge base & Blogs for festival muhurats and panchang queries.
   - Astropedia: Free, unlimited general astrology questions.
   - 1 Free personalized Vaani question to demonstrate the accuracy of chart-based AI.

2. **Mid Funnel (Micro-Transactions / Non-expiring Packs):**
   - Question Packs (Silver, Gold, Diamond) for ongoing life queries.
   - Report Packs (Silver, Gold, Diamond) to unlock specific life domain deep-dives.

3. **High-Ticket Flagship (Artha Sutram):**
   - High-value specialized financial astrology product (₹9,499) positioned as an essential wealth blueprint with lifelong re-readability and WhatsApp delivery.
